BIOGRAPHY

Sharon Ngigi is a fan of the Zootopia franchise and has created a photo gallery to celebrate the release of Zootopia 2. The gallery features a collection of images from the first film, as well as concept art and promotional materials for the sequel. Fans can explore the gallery to get a glimpse of what to expect in Zootopia 2 and relive their favorite moments from the original movie.

CHARACTERS

Judy Hopps: The main protagonist of the film, a determined and optimistic rabbit who becomes the first bunny police officer in Zootopia. She is known for her resourcefulness and determination to solve cases.

<>Nick Wilde: A sly and street-smart fox who becomes Judy's partner and friend. He has a complicated past but ultimately helps Judy solve cases and navigate the challenges of Zootopia.

Gary de' snake: A menacing and powerful snake who serves as one of the main antagonists in the film. He is known for his cunning and ruthless nature.

Pawbert lynxley : A friendly and helpful lynx who assists Judy and Nick in their investigations.
