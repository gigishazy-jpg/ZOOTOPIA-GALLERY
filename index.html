THE ADVENTURE

Zootopia 2 involve character Judy Hopps and Nick Wilde as they embark on a new adventure to solve a mystery in the city of Zootopia. The story will explore themes of friendship, teamwork, and overcoming challenges as they navigate through various obstacles and uncover secrets that threaten the harmony of their city. Fans can expect a mix of humor, action, and heartwarming moments as Judy and Nick work together to save Zootopia once again.

CHARACTERS

Judy Hopps

Judy Hopps: The main protagonist, a determined and optimistic rabbit who becomes the first bunny police officer in Zootopia. She is known for her resourcefulness and determination to prove herself in a city dominated by larger animals.

Nick Wilde: A sly and street-smart fox who becomes Judy's partner and friend. He has a complicated past but ultimately helps Judy solve cases and navigate the challenges of Zootopia.

Gary de' snake: A menacing and powerful snake who serves as one of the main antagonists in the film. He is known for his cunning and ruthless nature.

Pawbert lynxley : A friendly and helpful lynx who assists Judy and Nick in their investigations.
